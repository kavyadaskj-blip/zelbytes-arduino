## Mounting Notes

- HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor was placed on a flat desk surface next to the Arduino Uno.
- Sensor was mounted horizontally with the transmitter and receiver facing the target directly.
- Target surface used for testing was a flat, solid object positioned in front of the sensor.
- Best results were obtained when the target surface was perpendicular (approximately 90°) to the sensor face.
- Measurements were validated using a ruler at 10 cm and 30 cm distances.
- Sensor and target were kept at the same height to minimize reflection errors.
- Reliable readings were obtained within the tested range.
- Objects closer than approximately 2 cm were within the sensor blind zone and may produce inaccurate readings.
- Angled, curved, or soft surfaces can reduce echo strength and affect measurement accuracy.
